# Qubators Miner — Four Levels of Opportunity Mapping

- **Date:** October 2026
- **Status:** Internal (with PRD / feasibility / users docs — not for the site)
- **Purpose:** Define each level of opportunity and score Qubators Miner against it, so effort goes where the return is.

## The four levels

| Level | Question it answers | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| L1 — Problem opportunity | Is the pain real, severe and frequent? | Demand |
| L2 — Solution opportunity | Does our product solve it better than alternatives? | Fit |
| L3 — Market opportunity | Who pays, how many, and how do we reach them? | Reach |
| L4 — Business opportunity | Can it sustain and scale without breaking? | Viability |

---

## L1 — Problem opportunity (Demand)

**Definition:** Evidence that a painful, frequent problem exists for a reachable group — independent of any solution.

- Crypto scams drain an estimated **~$17B globally in 2025** (Chainalysis 2026); a single Zambian scheme took **~$300M from 65,000 victims** (Interpol 2025); African reported cybercrime losses roughly **doubled in a year** ($192M → $484M).
- Beginners cannot explain hash rate, difficulty, halving or break-even — yet fake "cloud mining" pitches exploit exactly that gap, daily, on WhatsApp/Telegram/TikTok.
- **Score: HIGH.** Severe (life savings lost), frequent (daily exposure), growing. This is the foundation everything else rests on.

## L2 — Solution opportunity (Fit)

**Definition:** Proof that our specific product relieves the pain better than what people use today (YouTube videos, blog posts, nothing).

- Interactivity beats passive content: lessons + simulator + quiz + certificate in one loop — no direct free equivalent in this niche.
- Offline-capable apps remove the data/connectivity barrier competitors ignore.
- Tamper-evident certificates + public verify page give proof-of-learning nobody else in the niche offers free.
- **Score: HIGH for learning; MEDIUM for trust** — trust becomes high only after server-side verification (v2.0). Until then, say so plainly (as the verify page does).

## L3 — Market opportunity (Reach)

**Definition:** Identifiable buyers/beneficiaries, reachable at a cost below their value.

- **Learners (millions, free):** reachable via WhatsApp shares, demo video, PWA link — near-zero acquisition cost, but they don't pay.
- **Institutions (hundreds–thousands of hubs/schools/cooperatives):** reachable via pilots, NYSC networks, church/youth programs — each deal covers hundreds of learners.
- **Donors/foundations:** reachable with the existing proposal + demo video + repo as due diligence.
- **Score: MEDIUM-HIGH.** Audience is large and reachable; paying segments (institutions, donors, certificate buyers) are narrower but high-value. The gap is discoverability — no Play Store presence yet.

## L4 — Business opportunity (Viability)

**Definition:** A model where revenue (or funding) covers costs and scales without linear cost growth.

- Costs are near-zero and flat: static hosting (free), cloud builds (free tier), no backend, no staff.
- Revenue levers, sequenced: verified certificates (₦500–₦2,000) → institution term licenses → grants; core learning stays free so growth never hits a paywall.
- Defensibility comes from brand + certificate trust + curriculum depth, not technology — so speed of execution and partnerships matter more than secrecy.
- **Score: MEDIUM today, HIGH after verification + analytics ship** (the two unlocks for charging and for proving impact to funders).

## Summary map

| Level | Score | Strongest evidence | Biggest gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 Problem | HIGH | $17B scam losses; daily exposure | — |
| L2 Solution | HIGH/MEDIUM | Only interactive + certifying niche tool; trust pending v2.0 | Server-side verification |
| L3 Market | MEDIUM-HIGH | Huge free audience; valuable narrow payers | Discoverability (no store listing) |
| L4 Business | MEDIUM → HIGH | ~zero costs; sequenced levers | Verification + analytics first |

**Reading:** Opportunity is validated at every level; nothing scores LOW. Priority order for effort: verification page ✅ (done) → analytics + pilots → Play Store → v2.0 registry. That sequence converts today's MEDIUMs into HIGHs.
