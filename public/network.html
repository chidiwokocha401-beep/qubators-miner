Stage 3: Network Effects

Difficulty adjustment & pool vs. solo

1. Difficulty adjustment

Bitcoin targets one block every ~10 minutes. Every 2016 blocks (~2 weeks), difficulty adjusts up or down based on how much total hash rate joined or left the network — keeping block time roughly constant no matter how many miners show up.

2. Pool vs. solo mining

Solo mining means you only get paid if you find a block — rare and unpredictable for small miners. A pool combines many miners' hash rate; when the pool wins, the reward is split proportionally — small, steady payouts instead of a rare jackpot.
