📖 What is Qubators Miner?

Qubators Miner is a free, interactive learning app that teaches how cloud mining really works — renting hash power from remote data centres instead of buying noisy machines for your home. Learners explore lessons, experiment with a profit simulator, pass a quiz, and earn a personalized Certificate of Completion showing their name, gender title (Mr./Ms.), score and grade.

🎯 Mission

Make Bitcoin-mining knowledge free and hands-on for everyone — while teaching people to recognize and avoid fake cloud-mining scams.

⚠️ Important

Educational simulation only. No real Bitcoin is mined, earned or exchanged. No real money is involved anywhere in the app.

🚀 How a learner uses it

  • Step 1 — Sign up: create a free account (name, Male/Female, email, password).
  • Step 2 — Learn: open the 4 lessons and mark each complete.
  • Step 3 — Simulate: drag sliders and discover what makes mining profitable — or not.
  • Step 4 — Quiz: answer 8 questions, score 50%+ to pass.
  • Step 5 — Certificate: auto-filled with name, title, gender and score — issue, print, share.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is this real Bitcoin mining?

No. It is an educational simulator. Nothing is mined and no cryptocurrency changes hands.

Does it cost anything?

No — the app, lessons, quiz and certificate are 100% free.

How do I earn the certificate?

Sign up, score at least 50% in the quiz, then open the Certificate tab and tap Issue Certificate. It prints your name (Mr./Ms.), gender, score, grade and date.

Does it work offline?

Yes — the installed PWA and the Windows/macOS apps work offline after the first load.

Is my login data safe?

Accounts are stored only in your own browser/device (demo-grade login). Nothing is sent to a server.
