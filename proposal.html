1Executive summary

Interest in Bitcoin mining is global, but honest, beginner-friendly education is scarce — and fake "cloud mining" scams drain victims daily. Qubators Miner is a free, interactive, offline-capable app that teaches how cloud mining truly works, lets learners simulate contracts with zero real money, tests them with a quiz, and issues a personalized Certificate of Completion (name, Mr./Ms. title, gender, score, grade). It is live today on four platforms, and this proposal invites partners to help scale it.

2The problem

  • Knowledge gap: most beginners cannot explain hash rate, difficulty, halving or break-even — yet these decide profit or loss.
  • Scam epidemic: fake cloud-mining sites promise "guaranteed daily profit," lock withdrawals behind "upgrade fees," and run Ponzi-style referral payouts.
  • Existing resources are text-heavy, technical, or tied to selling hardware/contracts — not neutral, hands-on learning.

3The solution

A neutral, interactive school for cloud mining that runs on any device:

  • Learn — 4 guided lessons with progress tracking.
  • Simulate — live profitability playground (hash rate, fees, BTC price, duration) with animated rigs and verdicts.
  • Prove — 8-question quiz, 50% pass mark, instant feedback.
  • Certify — printable certificate with name, gender title, score, grade, date and ID.
  • Stay safe — scam red-flag training woven through every stage.

5Target users

  • Beginners curious about Bitcoin mining with no technical background.
  • Students & youth seeking free, verifiable digital skills (certificate included).
  • At-risk users encountering "invest and earn daily" schemes who need scam literacy.
  • Educators & communities needing a ready-made, offline-capable teaching tool.

6Sustainability model

The app is and remains free for learners. Sustainability is partnership-based:

  • Voluntary partnerships/support via the in-app Partner button (OPAY 8130522995).
  • Proposed (future): sponsored lesson slots from legitimate educators, verified-certificate issuance for institutions, and optional translations — all clearly labeled, never paywalling core learning.
